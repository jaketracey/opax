# ABS annual GFS numerical-table rights

Checked 17 October 2026 Melbourne. This finding covers the 81 XLSX files linked from the [2024–25 release](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/government/government-finance-statistics-annual/2024-25), released 21 April 2026. Each file's URL, SHA-256, snapshot, sheets and located copyright notices are recorded in the [inventory](../../../data/staged/abs-gfs/inventory.json) and [receipts](../../../data/staged/abs-gfs/receipts.json).

The [ABS website copyright grant](https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer#copyright-and-creative-commons) applies CC BY 4.0 to website material, excluding the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, ABS logo, trade marks, microdata, third-party content and specified artwork/branding. The downloaded numerical GFS tables are ABS-produced material. They are neither microdata nor a customised client report.

All cells on every worksheet, including Contents, table footers and notes, were inspected for copyright/licensing/permission notices before parsing numerical observations. The only distinct rights notice across the 81 files is **© Commonwealth of Australia 2026**; every inventory entry retains its sheet/cell occurrences. No workbook contains a narrower grant, third-party rights notice or reuse exception. The numerical cells are cleared under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). No workbook is held. Branding and other excluded material are not reproduced.

ABS's former attribution link returns a same-host 302 to its current [citation guide](https://www.abs.gov.au/how-cite-abs-sources). The guide calls for the ABS author, reference period, title linked to the dated issue URL, and access date; spreadsheet citations also identify the spreadsheet title. OPAX shows the workbook/cell link with each value and keeps the release, snapshot and licence beside each block. The source section uses:

> Australian Bureau of Statistics (2024–25), Government Finance Statistics, Annual, accessed 17 October 2026. © Commonwealth of Australia 2026.

The title links to the dated release, rather than `latest-release`. Changes are disclosed: selected published cells are reformatted as a tidy series; display amounts use lowercase m/bn, while original AUD million values are retained; OPAX calculates no government, jurisdiction or sector total. This wording credits ABS and does not suggest ABS endorsement.

A newly added file requires its own notes/rights review and source hash. An unreviewed, restricted, challenged or held file fails the staging and serving gates and cannot enter the page, hydration payload or CSV.
