# Recent bill summaries, 8 September 2026

Added source-checked summaries for the 12 bills introduced since 1 August 2026
that did not have a publishable summary. All 28 bills in that period now have
summaries; the full registry has 1,710. Existing summaries were preserved.

Covered keys: r7535, r7536, r7537, s1512, s1514, r7534, r7523, s1502, s1503,
r7530, r7524 and r7526 (all prefixed `au-federal-`).

Each summary has three sentences, three to six factual changes and affected
groups, describes the proposal in the linked memorandum and retains model
attribution. The source publication date is recorded as `as_of`. Claims were
checked against the outline and relevant clause explanations, including the
firearms exclusions, departure-charge transition, court-order safeguards and
companion-bill boundaries. The automated-decision bill's memorandum has
inconsistent commencement cross-references, so the summary does not assert a
specific delayed commencement period.

The older OpenRouter generation key was unavailable. These summaries were
written and reviewed in Codex; provenance records `GPT-6 (Codex)` / `OpenAI`,
not the previous DeepSeek model. No OpenRouter generation request was sent.
This was model review, not a human or legal review.

Private source text, source hashes, review sections, the submitted payloads and
previous summary rows are retained on desktop at
`~/.cache/autoresearch/recent-bill-summaries-20260908/`. Full source text is not
published. Earlier flagged versions are preserved in the database. The public
files use the canonical exporter's summary projection and retain all existing
speech briefs and relationship data.

Bill-list summary-availability labels and filters remain absent. Updated bill
resources are upserted into the knowledge box and the response-cache epoch is
advanced when releasing the summaries.
