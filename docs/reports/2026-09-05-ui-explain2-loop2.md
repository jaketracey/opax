# Loop 2 critique — destinations, record and limits

Screens reviewed: every step for the Westpac donor page and Liberal party page at 390×844 and 1280×900, using WebGL through ANGLE/SwiftShader. Step 4 used four intercepted local streams across the capture matrix; no paid ask was made.

1. **Who gives**
   - The receipt clock still makes Westpac's disclosed activity feel like hundreds of individual filings, while the Liberal view makes the hierarchy of its contributing industries visible before any total is read.
   - Still imperfect: seven small destination labels make the Westpac side denser than the Liberal side, and the 1998 clock label reads a little detached from the ring on desktop.
2. **How much, and when**
   - The completed histogram shows that the same lifetime total can have a single exceptional year or a sustained sequence of large years; election ticks make that timing comparable to electoral cycles.
   - Still off on phone: the 2025 counter is the loudest object in the plate even when the final year's value is zero or tiny, and the election labels are only just large enough.
3. **Where it lands**
   - Westpac becomes seven share-width ribbons landing on party-coloured rings; Liberal reverses the reading into seven industry-coloured tributaries, while the outer gauge shows that the mapped donors account for 38.8% of the party's disclosed receipts.
   - Initial critique: the gauge sentence collapsed into a tall label block and the ribbons encoded magnitude but not direction. Fixed before the loop ended with one compact gauge note and arrow tips batched into the existing ribbon geometry. The smallest ribbons still converge tightly at 390px and need final spacing work.
4. **What parliament said**
   - Four dated, party-edged paper cards place the cited speeches on the 1993–2026 record, and hovering or focusing the first source visibly enlarges its matching card while muting the others.
   - Still vulnerable: real answers can return six citations in the same year, so the fixed three-lane rise can still make date labels collide; the phone framing also scrolls the narrative heading away when the harness focuses a citation.
5. **What this cannot show**
   - The disclosure floor now occupies more vertical weight than the reported histogram, while a second, lower arc is crossed out and labelled to keep state and federal totals separate.
   - Still slightly diagrammatic: the crossed arc's label and its X are visually separated, and the wide hatch band could use less contrast so it reads as absence rather than another measured quantity.

Worst two loop-2 findings fixed: the compressed gauge label and directionless split ribbons. Loop 3 will concentrate on collision handling, phone framing, typographic hierarchy and the relative density of all five end states.
