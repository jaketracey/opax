Each new byline resets identity rather than inheriting a previous speaker. The corrected parser reproduces **927 resolved out of 1,248 turns (74.3%)**, compared with 61.6% previously, on the same full cached comparison cohort. Normal publication excludes repair-owned speech IDs, including failed retries and text PATCH candidates.
