# Victorian election hub: export audit, 10 October 2026

Publication remains disabled pending Jake’s electoral-matter authorisation and
correction-contact decisions. The hub contains 88 Assembly districts, 8 Council
regions, 128 validated roster members and 97 canonical pages. The underlying vote and roster exports are unchanged.

Sources, licence terms, boundary changes and enablement instructions are in
[HUBS.md](HUBS.md). The official VEC lists match the existing OPAX state seats
exactly. There is no identified 2025 state redivision; the latest is the 2021
redivision, effective 1 November 2022. Eureka’s former name was Buninyong;
North-Eastern Metropolitan was Eastern Metropolitan. These historical changes
are sourced and do not imply a change between the 2022 and 2026 elections.

Data gaps apply to every constituency: no Victorian interests register, no state
grants, no exact mapping of federal electorates to state districts, and no
complete 2022–2026 term speech/division series. Victorian parliamentary records
have restricted copyright, without an open licence. Counts and links are served
from existing exports; no Hansard text is added. Donor records and candidate
lists are absent. Current VEC nominations close at noon on 9 November, rather
than 13 November in the superseded draft plan.
