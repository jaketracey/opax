# Navigation and IA rethink: Phase 1 proposal (12 October 2026)

Branch `web/nav-refactor`. This is the checkpoint: the inventory, the proposed grouping, the built mock-up and its screenshots. Nothing here is merged or deployed. The branch builds the proposal for real (`portal/public/nav.css`, `navigation.js`, the shell markup), so the screenshots are of the actual pages.

## 2. The proposed IA: five groups, plus Search and Ask

```text
Band, on every page:  [mark → Home]  Parliament ▾  People & places ▾  Money ▾  Topics & reports ▾  About ▾      [⌕ Search people, bills, topics  /]  [Ask OPAX]
                      (960px and narrower: [mark]  [⌕ Search]  [Ask]  [☰ Menu])

Parliament            In the chamber   Sitting weeks /sitting(/<week>) · Senate estimates /estimates/<id> [while a round is upcoming]
                      Laws             Bills /bills, /bill/<key> · Legislative instruments /instruments [ready.json gate]
                      Feature card     This week: "Sitting now · 12–15 Oct · House of Representatives and Senate" → /sitting/<week>
People & places       People           Parliamentarians · Parties · Declared interests /declared
                      Places           Electorates · Victorian election 2026 [flag gate]
                      Feature card     Victorian election 2026 (while the hub is published)
Money                 Political money  Money map /money · Political receipts · Donors · Campaigners & third parties
                      Public money     Government contracts /discover · Suppliers · Agencies · Grants /money/grants · Programs & places /connections
                      Feature card     The money map
Topics & reports      Topics           Topics A–Z · Interactive tools /explore
                      Reports          7 reports · Queensland audit reports /audit · All reports
About                 The project      About OPAX · Community
                      Using the record Methods & how to cite · Sources & coverage /stats

Footer (now a full site index): every link above, plus Expense definitions · Privacy · Support (footer only), source code and partner credits.
Reached in context, not from the menus: Search results (/ask?view=search), /chat, /doc/<id>, record pages, /map (from /money), /explore?game=<tool>, /today.
```

Moves from today:
- **Parliament is new.** It surfaces sitting weeks and estimates, and its "this week" card is built from the published sitting calendar (`/hubs/index.json`, 9 KB, fetched on the first menu open).
- **Ask & search is no longer a menu item.**
  - **Search** becomes a field-like control on every page. Pressing `/` opens it.
  - **Ask** becomes the band's one filled control on every page except Home, whose first block is already the question box.
- **Organisation directories move under Money:** donors, suppliers, agencies and campaigners.
- **Topics and Reports become one group,** with Queensland audit reports and the interactive tools.
- **Instruments moves under Parliament → Laws.**
- **Footer only:** Privacy, Support and Expense definitions leave the About menu. The footer becomes the site index.
- **The top level shrinks** from eight items plus an icon to five groups plus Search and Ask.

Every group, link, gate and blurb lives in one list in `navigation.js`. The menu bar, the sheet and the footer index are all drawn from it, so desktop and mobile can't drift again.

## 3. Breadcrumbs: one rule, one table

- **Rule:** Home › *the section page* (the link in the menu) › *the record*. The labels are exactly the menu's labels.
- **Money's own area** (receipts, contracts, grants, programs) keeps Money as its section, because `/money` is the hub with that area nav.
- **Groups are menus, not pages,** so they never appear as a step.
- **One crumb table in `navigation.js`** (Phase 2). The SPA's `setCrumbs` and the Worker's `crumbsHTML` both read from it. Every SSR page then prints the server trail too, so no-JS readers and crawlers get it.

| Route type | Proposed trail |
|---|---|
| Sitting week / estimates | Home › Sitting weeks › 12–15 Oct 2026 / Senate estimates, Oct 2026 |
| Bill / division | Home › Bills › *bill* (› Division) |

| Person / party / electorate | Home › Parliamentarians / Parties / Electorates › *name* |

## 4. Per-viewport design (built on the branch)

**Desktop (961px and wider; the 1440 shots)**
- **The band:**
  - A slimmer navy band: 64px plus the 2px bronze rule, down from about 80px.
  - A 44px mark, then the five group names in Public Sans 500/15px, soft white.
  - The current section is full white with a short bronze-bright bar under it.
- **The menu bar:**
  - Each group opens a full-width paper panel hung under the bronze rule.
  - A panel holds an intro column (group name in the serif heading role, plus one sentence), two columns of links (title plus a one-line blurb) and an optional feature card (radius 12, sunken paper).
  - The page behind dims to 22% navy, and a click there only closes.
  - Moving along the bar with a panel open swaps panels instantly, with no re-animation.
- **The right cluster:**
  - The search pill reads as a field ("Search people, bills, topics", with a `/` key hint) and opens a raised card with the field and its suggestions.
  - Ask OPAX is the one filled (paper) pill.
- **Between 961 and 1199px,** the search pill shortens to "Search".

**Tablet (641–960px; the 820 shots)**
- **The band:** the mark, the "Search" pill, Ask OPAX and "☰ Menu".
- **Menu** opens a sheet that drops from the top edge and stops where its content ends:
  - a large search field;
  - the navy "Ask OPAX" card;
  - "Start here" shortcuts;
  - then the five groups laid out open, two to a row.

**Mobile (640px and narrower; the 390 shots)**
- **The band:** the mark, the "Search" pill, an Ask icon and a Menu icon.
- **Menu** opens a sheet that rises from the bottom (rounded top corners, nearly full height). It holds:
  - a large search field and the Ask card;
  - "Start here" shortcuts as a horizontal row of chips: This sitting week (resolved to the current week), Find your electorate, Who funds whom, Latest bills, and Victorian election (gated);
  - the five groups as folding rows, each with a one-line description. The group holding the current page starts open and is marked with a bronze dot; its current link has a bronze edge.
- **"Start here" is the same for every reader.** Nothing about the reader's history is stored (see the decisions below).

**Keyboard and screen reader**
- **Menu bar:**
  - Each group is a disclosure button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
  - Down arrow opens a panel at its first link; Left and Right walk the bar.
  - Escape closes and returns focus to the button; tabbing out of a panel closes it.
  - Hover opens only on fine pointers, after a 90ms intent delay, and closes 180ms after the pointer leaves.
- **Links:** the link to the current page carries `aria-current="page"`; a section's parent link carries `aria-current="true"`.
- **The sheet:**
  - It is a modal `<dialog>`: the page behind is inert.
  - Tab is held inside it by an explicit cycle as well, so it behaves the same in every engine and can be tested in jsdom.
  - Escape and the backdrop run the same exit motion. Focus returns to whichever control opened it.
  - Group rows are buttons with `aria-expanded`.
- **Search:** the header search keeps its existing disclosure contract (Escape and outside clicks close it, focus returns to the pill).

**Motion (all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`)**
- Panels fade and drop 4px in 200ms (`--duration-standard`, `--ease-standard`).
- The phone sheet rises in 280ms and leaves in 220ms; the tablet sheet fades and drops 12px.
- Carets turn in 120ms.
- There are no ambient or looping effects in the band.

**Tokens:** none added. `nav.css` uses only the design-system tokens plus three local measures (`--nav-band`, `--nav-mark`, `--nav-pill`), scoped to `.site-header`.

## 8. Phase 2: as built (12 October 2026)

The decisions in section 6 were taken as recommended (control hub, for Jake). What was built, and where it differs from the proposal:

- **One list.** `portal/public/navigation.js` holds every page's one name and place. It draws the menu bar, the sheet, the footer index and (at stamp time, `scripts/stamp_assets.mjs`) the no-script fallbacks.
  - The Worker reads the same file through `portal/src/nav-model.mjs`, so its trails use the same names.
- **One name per page.** Menu, trail and short `<title>` now agree:
  - Declared interests · Sources & coverage · Expense definitions · Interactive tools · Government contracts · Programs & places · Money map · Legislative instruments · Queensland audit reports · About OPAX.
  - The Topics index keeps its own name, "Topics A–Z".
  - Report links use each report's own title ("Climate & Energy", "Media Ownership").
  - Search-descriptive titles are unchanged (bills, money, receipts, grants, search).
- **Trails.** Home › section page › record, from `trail()`.
  - The server writes the strip on every page (`.prerender-crumbs`), including the app's pages for readers without scripts. The app replaces it with its own (`setCrumbs(crumbsFor(…))`).
  - **Departure from section 3:** the Money pages have no "Money" step, because no page is called "Money". Political receipts, Government contracts, Grants and Programs & places are sections in their own right; a grant recipient is Home › Grants › recipient.
  - The Victorian hub uses the standard trail instead of its own; Community has one.
  - A donor's trail names the donor only where the page does (an indexable, confident match); a withheld donor's page and any placeholder stop at Donors.
  - A division sits under its bill (Bills › *bill* › Division) or, with no bill, under Search, as the app draws it; never under its first speaker's words.
- **Gates.**
  - The Worker marks published optional sections on `<html data-nav-gates>`, so the election link is drawn at load, with no request.
  - Estimates come from the sitting calendar, fetched on the first menu open.
  - The instruments link keeps its `ready.json` check.
  - Closed gates draw empty slots, so there is no address in the page.
- **Privacy** is in the footer index on every page and in the sheet's last line.
  - The full conversation page, which drops the footer, carries it in its head.
  - Saved conversations link to it, as do community sign-in, voice and account deletion (as before).
- **CSS.** `nav.css` draws the band alone and adds no tokens. Every band control is 44px on touch widths.
  - The old rules it supersedes in `style.css` are listed, with the check that proves it, in [nav-dead-selectors.md](nav-dead-selectors.md).
- **Structured data.** Every server-written page's BreadcrumbList is built from the same trail as its visible strip (`crumbSteps`), so the two cannot differ; `/community` gets one too. Pages with no visible trail (home, the full-screen map) keep the schema module's default.
- **Tests** (`portal/test/navigation.test.mjs`):
  - every page kind's way in;
  - every menu link served;
  - the server trail per route type;
  - the app's trails all going through the list;
  - static fallbacks;
  - the gate flag;
  - the sheet's focus cycle and Escape (with and without reduced motion);
  - the menu bar's keyboard model;
  - "/" not firing in fields;
  - reduced motion in `nav.css`;
  - the privacy links.
