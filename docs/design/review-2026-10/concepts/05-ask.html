David Pocock's housing-affordability proposals centre on using surplus Commonwealth land to require affordable housing in residential developments.

He proposes a standard obligation on the Commonwealth and its agencies to include affordable housing when surplus Commonwealth land is disposed of to private entities by sale or long lease [1]. Under the proposal, a proportion of such developments would normally be reserved for qualifying, income-eligible Australians at below-market rents [1].

Keep

  • ✓The question, the answer with numbered citations, the machine label, cited and retrieved sources, the people named (portrait, party dot and name), follow-ups, sharing, and the "Understood as" line when OPAX rewrote a follow-up (one fine line under the heading).

Merge or demote

  1. 1Utility rows. Retrieved records, Dates in the record and Share answer stack above the question. After the answer there is one "Sources" row (a sheet: cited first, then retrieved, then the dates in the record); Share and Copy go to ⋯.
  2. 2"Viewed 8 Oct 2026" joins the sources sheet as the answer's as-at.
  3. 3Question shown twice ("High", then "Understood as …"). The rewrite becomes the heading; "You asked: High" is a fine line under it.
  4. 5Machine label. "Machine-written from the retrieved passages; not the record." plus an ⓘ becomes the pill (its sheet carries that sentence and the guidance).

Remove

  1. 4The "Answer" heading with its icon tile and ⓘ: the question is the heading.
  2. ·"From the record" as a separate row beside Sources (same destination).
  3. ·The second Composer bound to the same draft (AskScreen renders two); one composer docks above the tab bar.
