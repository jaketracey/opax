Keep

  • ✓Status as a word with its date, the title, In short with its machine label, the stage history, every division with its party splits (dot, name, bar, counts), What was said and What became law.
  • ✓"Sponsor not recorded" stays a distinct state, said once in the source sheet, not as a fake party chip ("· NOT RECORDED" on the web).

Merge or demote

  1. 2Meta. Introduced, sponsor and portfolio lines (three on iPhone; a stray leading "·" on the web) become one meta line.
  2. 3Actions. Follow and Ask about this are the two pills; Read the bill text, Share and Open on the web sit behind ⋯. The web's four buttons at the top and three at the foot become one row: Ask about this bill, then ⋯ (Search the record, Official bill home ↗).
  3. 6Stages. Nine stage rows, each with its own ↗, become a ruler and "All 9 stages" (links kept inside).
  4. 7Splits. Show the three largest parties; the rest and the question text sit behind one disclosure.

Remove

  1. 1The "Bill" kicker (the nav and breadcrumb already say where you are).
  2. 4"Machine summary" badge plus a two-line attribution with three source links: the pill opens them.
  3. 5A lone ⓘ above the title; its note joins the divisions' source sheet.
  4. 8Raw Hansard as the division title ("Sue Lines Pursuant to the order agreed to on 18 August…"); the whole circulated amendment and a speech printed above the bars.
