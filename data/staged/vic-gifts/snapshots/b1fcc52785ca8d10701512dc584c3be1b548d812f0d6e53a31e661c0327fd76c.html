Ministerial register of gifts, benefits and hospitality: February to June 2025

Ministers and parliamentary secretaries are required to disclose accepted gifts, benefits and hospitality over a threshold amount

On this page

  • Ministers
  • Parliamentary secretaries

Gifts, benefits and hospitality accepted by ministers and parliamentary secretaries between 1 February 2025 and 30 June 2025 that have been disclosed in ordinary returns to Parliament (available here) are published on the ministerial register of gifts, benefits and hospitality.

The register is published every six months, in line with Parliament's ordinary return cycle.
