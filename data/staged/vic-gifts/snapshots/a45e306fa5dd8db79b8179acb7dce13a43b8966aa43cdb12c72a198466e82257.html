On this page

Gifts, benefits and hospitality accepted by ministers and parliamentary secretaries between 1 July 2025 to 31 January 2026 that have been disclosed in ordinary returns to Parliament (available here) are published on the ministerial register of gifts, benefits and hospitality.

The register is published biannually.
