Short and long quotations require literal selected-source text. Bad clauses are repaired or removed while supported clauses and the existing uncited passage points survive. Tests: both 73% and 91% resolve to retained cited text. Ranking fallback names the leader, subject and period, then puts runners-up in one sentence and retains the published-selection limitation once. PAY-BEFORE retains the December 1999 boundary; a covered historical Senate request retains the actual undated-chamber explanation instead; year rows omit the whole two-year interval. The same fixed grader is used on both arms. Tests: bananas and the old mining template fail; missing comparison side/coverage fails; distinct metric claims survive.

The existing 15-second production primary research allowance reserves four seconds for writing. Name resolution, research, selection and composition share the first eleven seconds; the research timer has a small handoff margin, and the outer selection cap prevents late work from consuming the writing slice. The writer receives only the time remaining inside the original allowance and retains its cancellation, limiter, daily reservation and token caps. Primary and existing ordinary-fallback deadlines were not increased.
