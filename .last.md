## Audit and fixes

`scripts/research_eval_audit.mjs` runs offline over every payload. It extracts named-entity/recipient associations, metric/value/unit atoms, factual periods and literal quotations. Each atom records supporting selected evidence IDs. It verifies complete associations rather than token co-occurrence: owner on either side of a value, donor-to-recipient direction, party counts, salary dates, coverage decades and source speaker/date. Only a unique selected speaker may use a first-and-last-name alias; donor names are not fuzzily merged. Requested dates in refusals are marked separately as request context, not asserted facts. Exact quotations and malformed quotation marks are checked. The audit neither calls a model nor fetches missing evidence.

Final new audit: **0 unsupported or misattributed extracted atoms across 50 payloads**, covering 454 atoms (160 entity, 131 figure, 158 factual-period, 5 quotation and 0 request-context atoms).

The frozen audit of the original saved review-fix1 payloads is retained in `acceptance/audit-before.json`. It identified unverified claims in GOLD-M03 and RESEARCH-H01. Missing support is treated as a failure, even when a truncated excerpt might omit relevant context; it is not certified as a fabrication. Unsupported clauses are removed while neighbouring supported claims remain. The final boundary uses the same pure audit for new loop answers and their ordinary fallbacks. Red/green tests cover invented recipients, swapped owners, wrong salary periods, another person’s percentage, short and long quotes, malformed quotes, metric/topic swaps, donor/recipient direction, party counts and decade-specific coverage.

Quote-aware clause repair prevents conjunctions inside a quotation from leaving half a quote. Punctuation edits preserve literal quotations and remap Unicode citation spans. Orphaned recipient fragments are removed. The source evidence is untouched. No case-ID exemption or gold expectation was introduced.

## Plain language

The final guard passes **50/50** new answers. Forbidden internal vocabulary and punctuation failures: **0/0**. JSON, SSE, ordinary fallback and cached replay share it. Regression tests include the exact unknown-range coverage-tool wording, space before a semicolon, comma before a full stop, Unicode citation rebasing, unchanged literal quotes and a clean completion paired with unsafe delta text. Existing visitor-copy rules in `plain-language.test.mjs` still pass.
