# Browsing surfaces: Android branch audit

Base: `6eaadd901` (`android/integration`, iOS 1.1.0 at `104b3a38d`).
Scope: Today, Your MP, seat choice, follows, leads, people, Explore (excluding
`quiz/GuessSlider.tsx`), public money, welcome, electorate outlines, Community,
About, profile split layouts and onboarding. This is a source audit, not a claim
that every device gate passed. Device evidence and gate results belong in the
lane's ignored QA report.

References: `docs/ANDROID-PARITY.md`, the Apple design direction and decisions,
the Today, Your MP, People, Explore and public-money specifications, and the
iOS 1.1.0 reference contact sheets. Android uses Public Sans, standard pushes,
native permission dialogs and scalable controls; iOS retains its original path.

Every executable `Platform.OS`, `Platform.select` and `isPad` branch in scope is
listed below. There are no `Platform.select` calls. Imports are not additional
branches. Paths are relative to `mobile/src/features/`.

`CachedPortrait.tsx`, `Electorate.tsx`, `Today.tsx` and `src/onboarding/**` have
no direct platform branches. The existing portrait implementation reserves a
blank 96 dp profile circle without initials, including cold and failed images.
The welcome keeps the version-1 seen flag, so updating from 1.0 does not replay
the welcome. Location requests still occur only after Use my location, keep
coordinates inside the matching function, treat Android coarse permission as
approximate and expose app settings after denial. Manual choice needs no grant.

Package A owns the four `GuessSlider.tsx` branches (native slider and the iOS
keyboard accessory). The quiz regression suite exercises their integration.
Navigation chrome, generic tab hiding, sheets, analytics, money-map code,
device scripts and Maestro files are not changed by this lane.

The changed shared branches retain iOS regression checks in
`tests/today-dots-render.test.tsx` (art position, scroll inset and clock),
`tests/android-photo-viewer.test.tsx` (the original iOS viewer's zoom and bar),
`tests/year-pictures-ui.test.tsx` (the original iOS image-error state), and
`tests/your-mp-screens.test.tsx` (the unchanged iOS forget prompt).
Android checks cover the reserved date space at 1.0 and 2.0, gesture geometry,
zoom and page actions, reduced-motion dismissal, modal Back and source return.
These component checks do not replace the required device flow and screenshot
matrix.
