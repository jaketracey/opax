# Loop 1 critique — receipts and time

Screens reviewed: every step for the Westpac donor page and Liberal party page at 390×844 and 1280×900, using WebGL through ANGLE/SwiftShader. Step 4 used an intercepted local answer; no paid ask was made.

1. **Who gives**
   - The picture adds the shape the prose cannot: Westpac's 1,091 receipts form a dense, year-positioned clock around one finance medallion, while the recipient rings show Labor and Liberal dominating the smaller destinations; the Liberal view instead makes finance the largest of seven incoming industry medallions.
   - Initial critique: Westpac's cloud was cropped at the left plate edge and the long Katter's label collided with neighbouring small-party labels. Fixed before the loop ended by tightening the clock radius without dropping points and shortening only the stage labels.
2. **How much, and when**
   - The picture makes the lumpy history visible: Westpac's isolated 2014 spike and Liberal's broader high plateau stand over the whole 1998–2025 ruler, with election years visibly threaded through both.
   - Still off-register: the large 2025 counter occupies too much of the phone's shallow stage and the election labels are near the smallest comfortable reading size; both need phone-specific polish in loop 3.
3. **Where it lands**
   - At this loop the picture says only that money moves left to right into a ring.
   - Still far too sparse: it has neither the destination split, share-width arcs, labels nor party-receipt gauge required for this step; this is the first loop-2 build target.
4. **What parliament said**
   - At this loop the continuing drift at least keeps the disclosed-money flow visibly separate from the prose answer.
   - Still far too sparse: there is no 1993–2026 record timeline, dated citation cards or text-to-card highlighting; this is the second loop-2 build target.
5. **What this cannot show**
   - The lighter arc suggests reduced certainty, but adds almost no information beyond the text.
   - Still far too sparse: the disclosure-threshold band, its label, and the crossed-out state/federal arc are all absent; this is the third loop-2 build target.
